# 🌍 Enterprise ESG Data Pipeline & Causal Strategy Analytics

Aligning Corporate Sustainability with Financial ROI via Causal Inference and Data Engineering.

## 📌 Executive Summary
In the era of the European Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) and the Twin Transition (Digital + Green), enterprises struggle to quantify the actual ROI of their sustainability and AI investments. 

This project bridges the gap between raw IoT/Supply Chain data and executive decision-making. It is an end-to-end data pipeline that not only aggregates carbon emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3) but uses Econometric Causal Inference to prove whether AI/BIS adoption genuinely reduces operating costs and carbon intensity.

## 🏗️ Architecture & Tech Stack
1. Data Engineering (SQL): - Designed an enterprise-grade Relational Database (PostgreSQL dialect) capturing Facilities, IoT Energy Logs, Supply Chain Shipments, and Financial Metrics.
   - Built advanced analytical queries using CTEs and Window Functions to track Quarter-over-Quarter (QoQ) Carbon Intensity.
2. Causal Analytics (Python):
   - Developed an Object-Oriented (OOP) simulation engine reflecting real-world corporate financial structures.
   - Implemented Fixed-Effects OLS Regression models (`statsmodels`) to isolate the *causal impact* of AI adoption from mere correlations.

## 📊 Simulated Business Impact
Based on the causal inference models executed in this environment, adopting an AI-driven Business Information System (BIS) yields:
* 12% Reduction in facility-level carbon footprint (optimizing IoT energy grids).
* 8% Decrease in operating costs.
* Statistically Significant improvements in Corporate Carbon Intensity (Emissions per Euro Revenue).
